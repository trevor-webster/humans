Question

Research framing
What is the story told among these human stories?
Can we find a familiar or fundamental human story?

Is there some kernel of truth to piece together from many modern books about humans and big history, including stories that may be incomplete or untrue?

Path Taken From commonality to comparison.

The motivation first looks like finding what is common, but finding what is common necessitates comparison. The tool should discover change between two stories so we can see what stands out as making a story human.

Data

Data origin and quality
Data wrangling: 1-, 2-, and 3-grams.

Use modern books in the sub-discipline of humans / big history, plus WikiText as a broad cultural baseline. Compare texts through n-gram counts rather than reading every book in full.

Path Taken Use WikiText as the cultural background.

Given limitations of Google Books, such as missing popularity, use Wikipedia/WikiText without pageviews. The featured-list filter gives a tractable popular-cultural baseline, while obscure material is partly filtered out.

Sub-decision: books were selected for human history, anthropology, and cultural visibility, enough to plausibly sit on a bookshelf. Limitation: some books are anthropology more than big history.

Method

Why this method

Book v Book

What remains distinctive when the reference is another human-history book?

Wiki v Book

Which book-side phrases rise above broad background language?

Wiki v Humans

Useful baseline, but only kept if it says something different from wiki-v-book.

Path Taken Why allotax?

From a big-picture view, allotax makes it immediate which words are comparatively important to each story. It is an instrument of importance: types and ranks, not sentiment, causality, or narrative time.

Quantifiable argument: each word or phrase has a rank in each system and an RTD contribution toward the difference between systems. The diamond, contour lines, word list, and matrices are different views of the same measured comparison.

Why to How

Method answers question
How can a story be pulled out without time?

It may seem impossible to pull a story out without narrative time, but it is like pulling the logline out of a plot: what stayed core, what became turbulent, and what characters of the story keep resurfacing?

Path Taken Contrast is where the story appears.

Schismogenesis suggests humans define stories by contrast. A neutral ground may not display much; where the stories decide to differ may reveal where the important human story is.

Sub-decision: why rank?

In each system there is an ordering of what is most important. The claim is not "these words felt important"; it is "these words repeatedly contributed most to the measured difference between systems."

Paths Tried, Parked, or Used as Limitations

Tempting because the question says "story", but word importance gave a clearer first instrument.

Google Books / popularity

Useful idea, but not clean enough for this pass.

Sentiment / temporal instrument

Not the question being answered here.

  • no causality claim

Two whys structure

Keep these as separate poster cards so the method justification does not blur.

  • Why allotax: why this tool rather than sentiment, story arc, or a new Zipf comparison.
  • Why to how: how comparative importance becomes evidence for a human story.
  • Why rank: ranks encode what is important to each system before comparing systems.
  • Weaknesses: no narrative time, no causality, imperfect baseline, mixed book genres.
