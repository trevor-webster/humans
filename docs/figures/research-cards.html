Research question

Many stories on humans

Is there a familiar or basic human plot? What are the different stories getting told, basically?

  • To make sense of all them, big picture.
  • Not the time to read them all.
  • The question asks what stands out as the human narrative.

Method

Common or different?

To find what stands out as the human narrative, the method has to ask how to answer the question.

  • Different: where do they differ, even with the same components?
  • Common: do the same ideas emerge for the humans stories?
  • Either way, finding what is common or different asks for comparison.
Data

Popular books

Books were selected for human history or anthropology, popular enough to sit on anyone's bookshelf. Sapiens is the clear bestseller example.

Weakness: this is not an extensive sample, but the justification is the Zipf, rich-get-richer argument that much of the cultural influence in the human story can be found in popular-ish books.

Data

Broad cultural baseline corpus

  • WikiText dataset of verified Good and Featured articles.
  • Probably passes a filter for popularity.
  • Why not Google Books: not a good measure of cultural popularity.
  • Google Books also biases toward scientific literature.
Why allotax

A tool for comparison

Allotax is the tool or method that answers how to compare.

  • It gives a big-picture view of how different two systems are.
  • It brings out which components are important to each story, relative to another.
  • It quantifies the comparison: what is important to a system, traceable to what component was measured.
  • Measurements can be used at scale for further conclusions.
Why not

Not sentiment

We were not looking for an overall measurement of sentiment.

The method is about importance in size, not sentiment. It asks what becomes important to the measured comparison.

Weaknesses

No narrative time

  • No narrative, no time element considered.
  • Narrative time, or how the author ordered discussion, was not the focus.
  • It may still be possible to get a picture of story without time.
  • Maybe it is like pulling a logline out of a plot.
  • A chronological time method had not been worked out.
Weaknesses

No causality

  • The method does not make a causal claim.
  • It does not verify authors' claims directly using other disciplines.
  • It is a method for comparative language, not proof that the books are right.
How concretely

3-gram and alpha

  • Use 3-gram for descriptive power, from inspection.
  • Use alpha = 0 philosophically.
  • It did not seem helpful to emphasize function words.
  • Visually, there was no best alpha.
